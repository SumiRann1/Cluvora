About Us

The Council of Student Affairs (CoSA) and its Science and Technology Council at IIT Bhilai stand at the forefront of nurturing student innovation, technical proficiency, and collaborative leadership. Operating from our permanent campus in Kutelabhata, Bhilai, the council provides students with state-of-the-art infrastructure, mentorship, and resources needed to translate inventive concepts into real-world applications across a comprehensive range of student bodies.

With an enduring focus on fostering creativity, hands-on engineering, and scientific temperament, CoSA plays a pivotal role in organizing technical workshops, hackathons, open-source initiatives such as CodePeak, and competitive contingents for the Inter-IIT Tech Meet and BAJA SAEINDIA. From competitive coding and artificial intelligence to robotics and automotive design, the council serves as the vibrant hub for student-driven innovation at IIT Bhilai.
